# Rejected: the Choir Militant were men with wings glued on

All four angel cards came back as the same thing — a soldier in plate, standing three-quarter, weight even on
both feet, with a symmetrical pair of large pale feathered wings spread behind the shoulders. Put the four side
by side and they are one character in four costumes. The faction is supposed to read as something built to a
purpose and sent out under orders; what it read as was a knight with a costume department.

Three specific failures:

- **Human where it counts.** Head on a neck in the usual place, two arms, two legs, one pair of wings, heroic
  proportions. Nothing about the body said *not a person*. The Warding Seraph even had a face-shaped helm sat
  at eye height, so the eye went to it and found a man.
- **The wings were an accessory.** A matched pair behind the back is the single most human-compatible way to
  add wings — it changes the outline hardly at all, which is why every card still had the same footprint.
- **Too pale and too clean to be half-moulted.** Near-white feathers and light armour read as celestial in
  spite of the grime, which is exactly what the faction is not.

The replacements are not people. Each states its own limb count in place of the Global Style Block's *no extra
limbs*, and each has no face anywhere: a sealed collar with nothing above it, an empty riveted socket between
raised shoulder plates, a wing folded permanently over the gap, an open hollow collar you can see down into.
Wings come in wrong counts from wrong sockets — one growing out of the top of a torso like a mast, a pair that
*are* the forearms, a violently one-sided set, a chest pair worn like a second breastplate — so no two of the
four have the same outline. Details of what worked on the model are in `art/prompts/STYLE_GUIDE.md`.
