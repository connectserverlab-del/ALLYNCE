# Rejected: three companies that were each the same man four times

Held against a contact sheet of the whole roster, these twelve cards were the worst of a problem that runs
through the first pass: the model's default figure is a standing three-quarter human, weight even on both feet,
arms down, weapon vertical, and almost nothing in a costume description pulls it off that pose. Costume does
not survive to card size. The outline does, and these outlines were identical.

**Cobalt Conclave** — the Magister, the Stormglass Wizard and the Cobalt Line Mage were three standing men
holding a stave upright in the right hand at the same height. Only the Ward Captain differed, and only because
he had a shield.

**Half-Born Host** — the worst of the three. Four bronze-armoured men of the same height and mass, planted the
same way, each with an upright shaft. The Sovereign and the Oathkeeper were close enough to be interchangeable
at card size; the levy had the same footprint as its commander, which is precisely backwards.

**Cutpurse Court** — four long grey coats standing in a row, arms at the sides, differing mainly in whether the
hood was up. A levy pickpocket and the commander of the company were the same height and the same width.

Two cutout defects were archived along with them rather than patched: a white pocket inside the Azure Seal
Magister's banner and another under the Garrote Adept's arm.

The replacements are built as groups, not as cards. Within each company of four, no two now share an answer on
height and mass, stance and weight, how the weapon breaks the outline, the head, or what hangs off the body —
a crouch, a wide brace, a long low diagonal and a horizontal bar across the shoulders in one; a run, a kneel, a
horizontal swing and a narrow vertical column in another; a tiny coil, a closed narrow column, a tall twist and
a wide cluttered mass in the third. The prompt wording that produced each is recorded in the subject blocks in
`art/prompts/UNIT_PROMPTS.md` so the variety is written down rather than rolled for.
